RAG and Knowledge Systems

Day 22 — What is RAG?

RAG retrieves external knowledge and places the most useful evidence into the model's context before generation. The model is only the last step of the pipeline.

Simple example

A policy assistant searches the latest approved policy documents, sends the relevant passages to the model, and cites them in the answer.

Trade-offs

  • More retrieved context can add noise.
  • Retrieval improves freshness but adds latency and infrastructure.
  • A strong generator cannot recover evidence that was never retrieved.

Production readiness checks

  • Measure retrieval and answer quality separately.
  • Keep source metadata and timestamps.
  • Enforce access filters in retrieval.
  • Evaluate with real query slices.
