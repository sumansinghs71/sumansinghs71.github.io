Day 25 — Vector Databases

A vector database stores vectors and finds approximate nearest neighbors quickly. In production, filtering, updates, recall and operational cost matter as much as raw search speed.

Simple example

A product assistant searches millions of item embeddings but filters results to the user's region and catalog permissions.

Trade-offs

  • Approximate search trades perfect recall for speed.
  • High-recall indexes use more memory.
  • Frequent updates can complicate index maintenance.

Production readiness checks

  • Measure recall@k on a labeled set.
  • Test filtered search behavior.
  • Plan reindex and backup procedures.
  • Monitor index size, latency and update lag.
