Workflow Orchestration

Day 44 — Durable Execution

Durable execution means workflow progress survives process crashes, deploys and machine restarts. Important state lives outside worker memory.

Simple example

A six-hour reconciliation continues after the worker pod restarts because completed steps and pending work are persisted.

Trade-offs

  • Durability adds storage and orchestration complexity.
  • Replaying work requires side-effect safety.
  • External systems may not be transactionally consistent with your state.

Production readiness checks

  • Persist state before losing ownership.
  • Use leases/heartbeats for workers.
  • Make side effects idempotent.
  • Test crash points between every major step.
