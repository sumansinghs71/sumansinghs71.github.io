Day 52 — What 50 Days of Writing About AI Agents Taught Me

After 50 days, the recurring lesson is that production agent quality comes from the system around the model: permissions, retrieval, state, recovery, evaluation and operations.

Simple example

A model upgrade helps less than fixing stale retrieval, duplicate side effects and missing traces.

Trade-offs

  • Breadth helps reveal system interactions.
  • Deep expertise still requires focused experiments.
  • Patterns should be revisited as models and runtimes change.

Production readiness checks

  • Keep a living architecture map.
  • Turn incidents into teaching artifacts.
  • Retire advice that no longer matches production reality.
  • Connect every lesson to an observable failure mode.
