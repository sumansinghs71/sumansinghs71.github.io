Day 35 — Webhooks for Agents

Webhooks push events when something happens instead of forcing an agent to poll. They turn background work into event-driven workflows.

Simple example

A vendor sends a webhook when document processing finishes; the agent resumes from the saved job state.

Trade-offs

  • Fast and efficient when delivery works.
  • Events can arrive twice or out of order.
  • Public endpoints need strong verification.

Production readiness checks

  • Verify signatures and timestamps.
  • Make handlers idempotent.
  • Store event IDs.
  • Define retry and dead-letter behavior.
