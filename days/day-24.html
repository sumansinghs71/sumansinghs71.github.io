Day 24 — Embeddings

Embeddings turn content into vectors so similar meaning can be found even when words differ. They are a retrieval representation, not a source of truth.

Simple example

'reset my password' can match documentation titled 'credential recovery' even without the same words.

Trade-offs

  • Better semantic matching can miss exact identifiers.
  • Embedding changes may require re-indexing.
  • Different languages and domains need validation.

Production readiness checks

  • Benchmark on domain queries.
  • Version the embedding model.
  • Normalize and store metadata consistently.
  • Keep keyword search for exact terms where needed.
