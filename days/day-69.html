Day 69 — Context Compaction

Context compaction decides what stays in the working set, what becomes a summary, what moves to durable storage, and what can safely leave active context.

Key idea

Compaction is not deletion. It is working-set management: preserve the state of the task while reducing what the model must reason over right now.

Simple example

After 50 steps, a long-running agent has a large conversation, tool logs, completed reasoning, files and partial results. A compactor retains the active goal, constraints, verified facts and next step; summarizes completed history; externalizes files, artifacts and checkpoints; and removes duplicates or stale chatter from the active prompt. Nothing important has to disappear from durable storage.

Trade-offs

  • Summaries are lossy and can remove a detail that matters later.
  • Compact too often and you add latency and churn; compact too late and the working set bloats.
  • A bad compaction policy can preserve a stale assumption while dropping fresh evidence.

Production readiness checks

  • Keep durable source material and provenance outside the prompt.
  • Version the compaction policy and every compacted checkpoint.
  • Retain goals, constraints, unresolved decisions and the next executable step.
  • Support rehydration: the agent can fetch original evidence when a summary is insufficient.
  • Regression-test that compaction preserves the decisions and invariants that matter.
