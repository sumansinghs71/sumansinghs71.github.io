Evals and Quality

Day 57 — RAG Evals

RAG evals measure the evidence path as well as the final answer. Retrieval can degrade while answer scores temporarily stay flat.

Simple example

The model answers correctly from prior knowledge even though the retriever returned irrelevant documents; retrieval evals reveal the hidden failure.

Trade-offs

  • Stage-level metrics improve diagnosis.
  • More metrics increase evaluation complexity.
  • Ground truth for retrieval can be incomplete.

Production readiness checks

  • Track recall/precision at k.
  • Check citation support.
  • Measure answer quality with and without retrieved context.
  • Slice results by source, filter and query type.
