Day 29 — Freshness and Stale Data

Freshness asks whether the system is using the newest valid information. A correct answer from last month may be wrong today.

Simple example

The policy changed this morning, but the vector index still serves yesterday's version.

Trade-offs

  • Frequent reindexing costs compute.
  • Immediate freshness can conflict with review/approval workflows.
  • Caching may preserve stale results.

Production readiness checks

  • Store source version and updated time.
  • Define freshness SLAs per source.
  • Invalidate caches on material changes.
  • Alert on ingestion and indexing lag.
