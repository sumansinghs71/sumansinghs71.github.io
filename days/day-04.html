Day 04 — Agent vs Workflow vs AI Agent

A workflow follows a known path. An agent chooses among possible paths. Use the least flexible design that still solves the problem.

Simple example

Invoice approval with fixed rules is a workflow. Investigating an ambiguous exception may need an agent.

Trade-offs

  • Workflows are predictable but less flexible.
  • Agents adapt but are harder to test exhaustively.
  • Mixing the two is often better than choosing only one.

Production readiness checks

  • Mark which steps are deterministic.
  • Use agents only where judgment is needed.
  • Keep irreversible actions behind workflow controls.
  • Measure whether autonomy actually improves outcomes.
