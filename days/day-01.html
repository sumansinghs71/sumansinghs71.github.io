Day 01 — AI Agents: what they are and why they matter

An agent is software that can choose a next step, use tools, observe the result, and continue toward a goal. The useful part is not the chat box; it is the ability to act inside a controlled system.

Simple example

A support agent reads a ticket, checks account history, drafts a reply, and opens a refund request only when policy allows it.

Trade-offs

  • More autonomy can remove manual work, but it also increases failure impact.
  • A capable model does not replace workflow controls.
  • Agents are a poor fit when the path is deterministic and stable.

Production readiness checks

  • Define the goal and allowed actions.
  • Put hard limits around tools and data.
  • Trace every important decision and action.
  • Have a clear fallback when the agent is unsure.
