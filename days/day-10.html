Day 10 — Agent Autonomy Levels

Autonomy level describes how much the agent may decide and act without a person. Increase it only when evidence supports the extra freedom.

Simple example

An assistant may draft a refund at low autonomy; at higher autonomy it can issue small refunds and only escalate large ones.

Trade-offs

  • Higher autonomy improves speed but raises risk.
  • Human review reduces risk but can become a bottleneck.
  • Different actions need different autonomy levels.

Production readiness checks

  • Classify actions by risk.
  • Define approval thresholds.
  • Recheck permissions at execution time.
  • Measure incidents by autonomy level.
