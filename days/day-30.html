Day 30 — Citations and Grounding

Citations connect an answer to the evidence used to support it. Grounding checks whether the cited evidence actually supports the claim.

Simple example

An agent says a fee is waived and links to the exact policy paragraph that states the exception.

Trade-offs

  • Citations add UI and retrieval work.
  • A citation can be present but irrelevant.
  • Some tasks use tools or calculations rather than documents.

Production readiness checks

  • Verify citation-to-claim support.
  • Keep stable source IDs.
  • Show enough context for a human to inspect.
  • Fail gracefully when evidence is insufficient.
