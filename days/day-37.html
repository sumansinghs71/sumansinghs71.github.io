Day 37 — Queues and Pub/Sub

Queues and Pub/Sub decouple slow or bursty work. They are essential when an agent should hand off work without waiting for it to finish.

Simple example

An agent submits 5,000 document jobs to a queue; workers process them safely while the user receives progress updates.

Trade-offs

  • Queues absorb spikes but add eventual consistency.
  • At-least-once delivery means duplicates are normal.
  • Ordering can limit parallelism.

Production readiness checks

  • Use idempotent consumers.
  • Monitor queue age, not just depth.
  • Configure retries and DLQs.
  • Carry trace IDs through messages.
