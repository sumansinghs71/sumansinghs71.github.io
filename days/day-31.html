Day 31 — RAG Failure Modes

RAG can fail at ingestion, chunking, indexing, retrieval, filtering, ranking, context assembly or generation. Treat it as a pipeline, not one model call.

Simple example

A wrong answer is traced to a stale index rather than blamed on the LLM.

Trade-offs

  • More components improve control but add operational surfaces.
  • End-to-end scores can hide which stage regressed.
  • Fixing generation may not help retrieval failures.

Production readiness checks

  • Trace document IDs through the pipeline.
  • Maintain stage-level evals.
  • Test stale, missing and conflicting documents.
  • Record retrieval candidates for failed queries.
