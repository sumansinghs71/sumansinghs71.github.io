Day 27 — Reranking

Reranking takes a small candidate set and scores it more carefully. It is often the quality step between fast retrieval and expensive generation.

Simple example

Retrieve 50 documents cheaply, rerank them with a cross-encoder, then send the best 5 to the model.

Trade-offs

  • Better ranking adds latency and compute.
  • Rerankers can overfit benchmark phrasing.
  • Reranking cannot recover documents missing from initial retrieval.

Production readiness checks

  • Measure retrieval recall before reranking.
  • Set top-k and top-n latency budgets.
  • Evaluate on difficult near-duplicate documents.
  • Use fallbacks when the reranker is unavailable.
