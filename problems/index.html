Start with
what broke.

Production AI failures rarely live in one layer. Start with the symptom, then trace the runtime, retrieval, identity, policy, capacity or dependency boundary that actually failed.

Use this like a field guide: every example is generalized and intentionally free of proprietary incident details. The goal is the reusable engineering pattern.
