Safety RuntimeProduction failure pattern

⛨The safety check failed because its own dependency was down.

Guardrails have availability dependencies too. The fail-open/fail-closed/degraded decision must be policy, not incident improvisation.

action→guardrail→dependency DOWN→mode?
Unavailable policy is different from denied policy.
Signals in production
  • Policy/guardrail calls fail because an upstream dependency is unavailable.
  • Low-risk reads and high-risk writes are blocked identically.
  • Operators consider bypassing the safety layer to restore service.
Failure boundary

The runtime cannot obtain a safety decision but must still decide what authority, if any, remains.

01

Why it happens

Safety services may depend on identity, policy stores, classification models or external APIs. If their outage mode is undefined, the product either stops unnecessarily or people create emergency bypasses under pressure.

02

Production-shaped example

A guardrail relies on an external identity/data service. That dependency fails. Read-only summarization and irreversible write actions now receive the same unavailable result even though their risk is very different.

What fooled the team
one global fail-open/fail-closed rulebypass as an operational shortcuttesting jailbreaks but not guardrail infrastructure failure
First 5 minutes
  1. Distinguish POLICY_DENIED from POLICY_UNAVAILABLE.
  2. Apply the pre-defined risk mode for the requested action class.
  3. Keep high-impact writes blocked unless an alternate authoritative control exists.
03

Controls

Prevent the failure, detect it early, then recover without making it worse.

PREVENT
  • Define outage policy by action risk: read, propose, reversible write, irreversible write.
  • Keep hard authorization enforcement in the runtime.
  • Cache only safe policy with explicit version and expiry semantics.
  • Minimize correlated dependencies between agent execution and authorization.
DETECT
  • Separate deny-rate from unavailable-rate.
  • Trace the exact dependency that prevented the decision.
  • Alert on degraded mode, bypass and cached-policy use.
RECOVER
  • Enter pre-defined degraded mode; do not improvise authority.
  • Revalidate deferred actions after recovery.
  • Automatically expire any emergency override and audit every use.
Prove it with failure injection
  • identity dependency offline
  • policy store offline
  • cache expires during outage
  • read vs irreversible write
  • emergency override auto-expiry

PRODUCTION INVARIANTLoss of a safety dependency must never silently grant more authority than the healthy system would allow.

The guardrail has a failure mode too. Design it before the incident.
